# Briqen product and internship research

## Product fit

Briqen MI helps marketers research creators and channels on Instagram and YouTube, understand why particular hooks and formats work, and turn those findings into scripts, storyboards, and video. A short product reel is a natural Creator Studio example. Start with an insight about a strong opening hook, carry it into an editable script, and let a marketer audition scene assets before deciding the final cut. The take-home can take the creative brief as an input. Creator discovery and content analysis sit upstream. [Briqen product overview](https://briqen.com/), [Founder’s Briqen MI launch post](https://www.producthunt.com/products/briqen-mi)

Briqen describes usage-based credits. Its separate BriqArc product publicly explains a hold, settlement, and release flow for generation credits. That gives the take-home a relevant product precedent for showing what a new generation costs and why Retry carries no second credit charge. Creator Studio’s billing rules are a design decision for the exercise. [Briqen product overview](https://briqen.com/), [BriqArc credit policy](https://arc.briqen.com/)

## Internship signals

The public syndicated listing for **Backend & Product Engineering Intern** asks applicants to describe a project, explain what they personally owned, and say why early-stage work appeals to them. It gives broad backend and product engineering responsibilities. I found no first-party detailed job description or published engineering stack for the exact role. The employer’s take-home is the first-party source for the workflow, constraints, and submission format. [Carrerlift role listing](https://www.carrerlift.in/jobs/briqen-ai-backend-product-engineering-intern-remote-2e4f3ebc)

Show both halves of the role through one workflow. For backend engineering, make callback correlation, credit settlement, and export inputs auditable. For product engineering, specify what the marketer can see and do during a long wait, a timeout, and a deliberate regeneration. Keep the design deployable as a small API and worker system a startup can operate.
